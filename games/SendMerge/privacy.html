This policy explains how the mobile game SendMerge (Korean title: 모래 게임, the “Game”) handles information. It applies to the current version offered on Google Play.

1. Developer

Developer: NamDanChu
Privacy contact: l9lettuce@gmail.com

Scores and settings stay on the device and are removed when the app is uninstalled. Other privacy questions can be sent to the email above.

2. Information we do not collect

The Game does not create an account. It does not collect your name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts, location, microphone, camera, advertising ID, or device identifiers. Scores are not sent to a server, and there is no online leaderboard.

3. Information that stays on the phone

Progress and settings are stored only on that phone. They are not sent to the developer or to any other company.

ItemWhat it isSent off the device
Best scoreThe highest score on that deviceNo

The “top percent” label shown during play is calculated on the device from that score. It is not a comparison with other people and it is not uploaded.

4. How it is used

This information is used only to show the score and settings again on the same phone. It is not used for advertising, sale, analytics, or sharing with other companies.

5. Sharing

The current version has no advertising module, analytics tool, sign-in, in-app purchase, or developer server. User data is not shared with or sold to advertising companies, analytics companies, or data brokers.

Installing and updating the app through Google Play is handled by Google under Google’s privacy policy. The Game does not receive or store that information.

6. Retention and deletion

The information exists only on the phone where the app is installed. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the app’s data in the phone settings, deletes it. The developer does not keep a copy.

7. Children

The Game does not collect personal information from children. It does not ask for age and it does not require an account. The developer does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Security and transfers

The developer does not move user information to a server or to another country. Scores and settings that remain on the phone are managed with that phone’s lock and by clearing the app data.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date on this page will change. If a future version collects data or shares it with a third party, this policy will be updated before that version is released on the store.
